## Codex task titles
- Once a concrete task is clear, use `$codex-thread-title-manager` to maintain the sidebar title. Default to `Project or domain｜Concrete primary task or deliverable`, add a version only when useful, and normally stay within 36 characters.
- Do not title from a greeting, raw URL, attachment name, an unspecified request, Skill/Agent name, or executor action.
- Rename only when the project, primary object, deliverable, or task boundary changes materially. Ordinary clarifications, revisions, and follow-up questions do not justify another rename. Re-evaluate once at a natural phase boundary.
- A user-specified or manually changed title is authoritative and must not be overwritten automatically. Preserve the current title when ownership is uncertain.
- Current-task automatic upkeep may run directly. Historical or batch renames must first show the complete `old title → new title` mapping and obtain explicit approval of that exact mapping.
- Title upkeep is ancillary and must not block the main task. Use only official task-title capabilities; never edit Codex databases, rollouts, caches, or application files.
